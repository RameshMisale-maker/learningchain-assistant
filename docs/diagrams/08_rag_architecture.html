Updated 23 Nov 2026 - blocks tagged UPDATED changed after the 7-phase upgrade: conversation ending and rating, one combined FAQ file, English-only replies, the senior-review fixes (webhook replay, rate limiter, topic filter) and the evaluation fixes (own-account check, vague questions, undocumented-term guard) and the 23 Nov full-evaluation fixes and the 24 Nov fixes (wrong assumptions corrected from the sources, no counted totals, a leading context slot for each part of a multi-part question) and the 26 Nov token budgets and closer-to-source answers (everyday words no longer declined, bare-topic clarification, both sides of a comparison retrieved, revoke vs recall).

How the assistant indexes content and answers a question after the seven-phase upgrade (19 Nov 2026), drawn from the current code. Blocks outlined in light blue with a NEW tag were added by the upgrade (P1-P7 = phase). Every new block sits behind a feature flag that is off by default; all of it runs on free components.

Current configuration

Products: sapphirus (default, company + products) · learningchain (products) LLM: Groq gpt-oss-120b → gpt-oss-20b (rate-limited model skipped until its limit resets; + optional free 2nd provider) Embeddings: bge-small-en-v1.5 (local) Search: dense 40 + BM25 40, relative fusion, multi-query Rerank: ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2, top 5 Database: SQLite / PostgreSQL, Alembic 0001-0008 Replies: English only (other languages understood) LearningChain FAQ: 107 approved answers (FAQ_Master + 10 modes, 1 file) Evaluation 20 Nov: 90% pass, recall@5 0.95, 0 hallucinated claims Cache: database table, 24 h, semantic ≥ 0.95 Eval gate: floor 0.85 · live − 0.02 · best − 0.05, fails closed Edge: nginx TLS 1.2/1.3, 20 req/min chat Backups: backup.sh nightly, 14 days Release: manual, no CI pipeline yet Breaker: 5 failures / 60 s → 120 s, LLM timeout 15 s Crawl: daily, tombstone after 2 misses
