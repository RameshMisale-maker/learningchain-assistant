# Sapphirus Systems Pvt Ltd - Company Overview

Source: public website https://sapphirus.in (About Us, Home, Management Team, Work Culture pages).

## About Sapphirus
Sapphirus Systems Pvt Ltd (Sapphirus) is a technology solutions company focused on turning emerging and enterprise
technologies into practical solutions for real-world challenges. Blockchain is its core technology capability,
complemented by Artificial Intelligence (AI), Cloud, Data & Analytics and application engineering.
Sapphirus works with enterprises, educational institutions and government organisations.
Its tagline is "Building Trust into Every Digital Interaction". Its positioning is "Make in India. Global Footprint." -
engineering is based in India, with experience across global markets.

## Purpose, Vision and Mission
- Purpose: "Building a Smarter, Trusted Digital Future" - helping organisations and institutions operate with greater
  trust, intelligence and confidence.
- Vision: "Turning Innovation into Meaningful Progress" - applying emerging and enterprise technologies to real-world
  challenges by combining innovation, engineering and collaboration to create sustainable value.
- Mission: "Building Technology That Matters" - making emerging and enterprise technologies practical, accessible and
  valuable.

## Values
Sapphirus's values are: Innovation with Purpose; Integrity (trust, transparency and accountability); Excellence
(quality across technology, engineering, delivery and support); Collaboration; Client Success; and Continuous Learning.

## Why Choose Sapphirus
- Make in India, Global Footprint.
- Forward-looking approach built on emerging technologies such as blockchain and AI.
- Proven experience with education and government organisations.
- Practical, business-first thinking.

## Capabilities and Engagement Models
Capability areas: Blockchain & Emerging Technologies; AI, Data & Analytics; Cloud & Digital Infrastructure;
Application & Software Engineering; Quality, DevOps & Delivery.
Engagement models: whether a client needs an individual specialist, additional expertise for an existing team or a
dedicated technology team, Sapphirus builds an engagement model around the client's requirements - including
project-based delivery, dedicated teams, modernisation programmes and ongoing support.
How Sapphirus works (delivery approach): Discover, Design, Build, Deploy & Scale.

## Industries Served
- Education: digitising exam records, secure digital credentials, QR-based transcript verification and student
  identity (IDSecure). Focus areas: digital credentials, academic verification, digital learning, institutional records.
- Government & Public Sector: work with police and public-sector organisations - integrating data into centralised
  dashboards and structured data systems, civil registration, digital records and municipal data analysis. Focus areas:
  identity verification, document verification, digital records, institutional systems.
- Technology & Enterprises: application modernisation, cloud, system integration and data analytics.
- Retail & Consumer Goods: product authenticity with LabelSecure, traceability and reducing counterfeit risk, supply
  chain visibility, AI & analytics.
