### What is a Blockchain-Secured QR Code?

A Blockchain-Secured QR Code is a QR code integrated with **blockchain technology** to ensure that the data it carries is immutable, transparent, and secure. Unlike traditional QR codes that can be easily copied or tampered with, blockchain-secured QR codes link to data stored on a decentralized ledger, making them virtually impossible to forge.

### How Does It Work?

**Data Encoding**: Information about the product or document is encoded into the QR code.

**Blockchain Integration**: This data is simultaneously recorded on a blockchain, ensuring it cannot be altered without detection.

**Verification**: When scanned, the QR code retrieves real-time data from the blockchain, confirming its authenticity.
