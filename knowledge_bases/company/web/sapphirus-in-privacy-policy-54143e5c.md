## 2. Our role

- **Data Fiduciary.** When you visit our website, contact us, ask for a demo, apply for a job or attend our events, we decide why and how your data is used.
- **Data Processor.** When a university, examination board, government department or company (our “client”) uses our platforms, the client is the Data Fiduciary. This covers issuing credentials, running exams, managing documents, ID cards or finance records, and delivering citizen services. We process that data only on the client’s documented instructions and under a written contract. Send requests about it to the client first; we will help the client respond.

## 3. Personal data we collect

- **Identity and contact:** name, email, phone, organisation, designation, address.
- **Account and login:** username, encrypted password, single sign-on tokens, role.
- **Data our clients give us to process:** student or candidate name, enrolment or roll number, programme, marks, certificates, photographs, ID card details, exam session data (including ProctAI images and video), finance records such as vouchers and receipts, citizen or property records, and Samvaad conversation transcripts.
- **Careers:** CV, qualifications, employment history.
- **Technical:** IP address, device and browser details, pages visited, logs.
- **Payments:** where a product portal collects a fee, such as a verification fee, a licensed payment gateway processes it. We do not store card numbers.

We collect only what the stated purpose needs. We do not ask for Aadhaar numbers unless the law requires it for a client’s service, and then only as the client directs.

**Blockchain records.** None of our platforms writes personal data to a blockchain. We anchor only a cryptographic hash of an encrypted record, with the issuer’s identity and a timestamp. The record itself stays with the institution or department, where it can be corrected, retained or erased under its own policy. This is how an unchangeable ledger and your right to erasure work together.

## 4. Why we use it

- **With your consent.** We ask through a clear notice that names the purpose: replying to enquiries, demos, newsletters, job applications.
- **Legitimate uses allowed by the DPDP Act.** Data you give us for a purpose you would expect, such as answering your message. Also compliance with law or court orders, and employment-related purposes.
- **For our clients.** Only the purposes in our contract with them.

Within those limits we use personal data to:

- deliver and support our platforms;
- verify credentials, identities, documents and products;
- authenticate users;
- protect our systems and detect fraud;
- communicate with you;
- meet legal duties;
- improve our products using aggregated or de-identified data.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use client data for advertising. AI models trained on or grounded in one client’s data are not reused for any other client or purpose without that client’s prior written approval.

You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: email privacy@sapphirus.in or use the link in our messages. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done.

## 5. Children’s data

Under the DPDP Act, a child is anyone under 18. Many students and candidates on our clients’ platforms are children.

- **Where we are the client’s processor,** the client obtains verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian, or relies on the exemptions the DPDP Rules give educational institutions for educational activities and safety. We follow the client’s instructions.
- **Where we are the Data Fiduciary,** we do not knowingly collect a child’s data without verifiable parental consent.
- We do not track children’s behaviour, monitor them for advertising, or target ads at them. ProctAI is used for university examinations. Candidates give their consent before each exam, and monitoring runs only during that exam session.

If you believe we hold a child’s data without proper consent, write to the Grievance Officer and we will act on it.

## 6. Who we share it with

- **Our clients:** the institution that issued your credential, ran your exam or holds your record.
- **Verifiers you choose:** when you share a credential, the employer or institution you share it with sees only what it needs to verify it.
- **Service providers working for us:** cloud hosting (Microsoft Azure, Central India region), email and SMS delivery, payment gateways, smart-card printing for ID Secure, and survey partners on Sutradhar projects. They are bound by contract to process data only on our instructions and to protect it. A list is available on request.
- **Authorities:** where the law, a court or a government agency lawfully requires it.
- **Business transfer:** if Sapphirus merges or is acquired, your data transfers with the same protections. We will tell affected clients first.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.

## 7. How we protect it

- **Technical controls:** encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access with least privilege, access logs, backups and business-continuity plans.
- **Logs:** we keep logs for at least one year to detect and investigate incidents, as the DPDP Rules require.
- **Certifications:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022, ISO 9001:2015, ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 and CMMI Level 3. Our systems are tested by CERT-In empanelled auditors.
- **Our processors:** we bind them to the same standards by contract.

If a breach affects your personal data:

- we tell you without delay what happened, the likely impact, what we are doing and what you can do;
- we notify the Data Protection Board of India without delay, with a detailed report within 72 hours;
- we report the incident to CERT-In within 6 hours, as Indian law requires;
- where we act for a client, we notify the client without delay and support its response.

No system is completely secure, but we work to keep the risk low.

## 8. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as the purpose needs it or the law requires, then erase it.

| Data | How long we keep it | 
| Website enquiries and demo requests | 24 months after our last contact with you | 
| Newsletter and event sign-ups | Until you unsubscribe | 
| Job applications | 12 months, unless you join us | 
| Website analytics | 14 months, in aggregated form | 
| Security and access logs | At least one year | 
| Data we process for a client | As set in our contract with that client. When the contract ends we delete or return it as the contract specifies | 

Backups we cannot erase at once are kept apart from live systems until they are deleted.

## 9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

- get a summary of the personal data we hold about you, how we use it and who we have shared it with;
- have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected, completed or updated;
- have your data erased when it is no longer needed, or when you withdraw consent, unless the law requires us to keep it;
- withdraw consent at any time;
- have a grievance addressed by our Grievance Officer;
- nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.

**How to ask.** Email privacy@sapphirus.in with “DPDP request” in the subject. We will verify your identity and reply within 30 days, and never later than 90 days. If an institution holds your data on our platform, contact the institution; if you write to us, we will pass your request to it.

The DPDP Act also asks you to give accurate information and not to file false or frivolous complaints.

## 11. Cookies

- **Essential cookies** keep the site and your login working; they cannot be switched off.
- **Analytics cookies** tell us, in aggregate, how the site is used. We set them only if you agree in the cookie banner.
- **Functional cookies** remember choices such as language.

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking or ad networks. You can change your choice in the banner at any time, or block cookies in your browser; blocking essential cookies may stop parts of the site working.

## 12. Where your data is stored

Government data stays in India: in a government cloud, State Data Centre, NIC, the department’s own infrastructure or an India region of a public cloud, as the department chooses. Our platforms are hosted on Microsoft Azure in the Central India region. This website is hosted by Site Ground Virginia (USA) ; enquiries sent through it are delivered to our email. Some service providers may access data from outside India; the DPDP Act allows this unless the Government restricts a country, and we follow any such restriction, our clients’ contracts and the safeguards in section 7.

## 13. Job applications

When you apply for a role through our website or a recruitment channel, we use what you send (contact details, CV, qualifications, experience) to assess you for that role and others that fit. Only authorised staff and recruitment partners involved in hiring can see it. We keep it for 12 months unless you join us or ask us to delete it sooner.

## 14. Third-party websites

Our website links to sites we do not run, including product sites such as learningchain.in and bsecured.in where those are operated separately. Those sites have their own privacy practices; please read their policies before sharing personal data there.
