## About LearningTree

LearningTree is a digital education platform portfolio designed to support essential academic activities, from learning delivery to examination administration and online assessment monitoring.

With dedicated solutions for learning management, examinations and proctoring, LearningTree helps institutions build more organized, efficient and dependable academic processes.
