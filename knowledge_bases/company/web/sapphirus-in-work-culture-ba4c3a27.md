## What we do

At Sapphirus, we believe that great technology begins with people. We are committed to creating a workplace where individuals can learn, collaborate, contribute and grow while building solutions that address real-world challenges.

Our culture encourages **continuous learning, collaboration, creativity and ownership**, giving our people opportunities to develop their capabilities and make meaningful contributions.

### Contribution. Collaboration. Creativity.

These principles shape how we work and grow together.

###### Contribution

We encourage every team member to take ownership of their responsibilities and contribute meaningfully to shared goals.

###### Collaboration

We believe strong outcomes come from working together, exchanging perspectives and supporting one another across teams.

###### Creativity

We encourage our people to think differently, explore new ideas and find better ways to solve business and technology challenges.

### Corporate Social Responsibility

At Sapphirus, we believe growth carries a responsibility to contribute positively to the communities around us.

Our social responsibility initiatives bring our people together around meaningful causes and reflect a culture that values **empathy, participation and giving back**.

## Turning a Celebration into an Opportunity to Give Back

Every year on **7 June**, Sapphirus marks our CEO’s birthday by spending time with children at an orphanage and supporting the community through meaningful activities.

Our employees participate in the initiative by engaging with the children, encouraging their creativity and talents, spending time together and contributing gifts and other support.

Over the years, this has become more than an annual celebration. It represents a tradition of bringing our people together to create moments of happiness and contribute beyond the workplace.

#### Supporting Learning and Opportunity

Education can create opportunities that last far beyond a single initiative.

As part of our broader community outlook, Sapphirus seeks opportunities to support children and educational development, helping create pathways for learning and future growth.
