# Learning Management System | Sapphirus Systems Pvt Ltd

Source: https://sapphirus.in/learning-management-system/
Last updated: 2026-11-09

LearningTree LMS is a digital learning management solution designed to help educational institutions organise, deliver and manage learning activities through a centralised platform.

It brings together course content, learning resources, student participation and academic progress information to support a more accessible and organised teaching and learning experience.

## Product Highlights

#### Course Management

Create and organise courses, learning modules and relevant academic resources.

#### Digital Content Delivery

Provide students with access to learning materials, documents and supported educational content.

#### Learner Management

Support student enrolment, course participation and access to assigned learning activities.

#### Assignments & Assessments

Manage relevant learning activities and assessments through supported digital workflows.

#### Learning Progress Tracking

Provide educators with visibility into student participation and learning progress.

#### Educator & Student Access

Enable role-appropriate access to learning resources and academic activities.

**Schools, colleges, universities, training centres and organisations delivering structured digital learning programmes.**

#### About LearningTree LMS

LearningTree LMS helps educational institutions manage teaching and learning activities through a structured digital environment.

By bringing course organisation, content delivery, learner participation and progress visibility together, the solution supports educators in managing academic activities while providing students with convenient access to learning resources.
