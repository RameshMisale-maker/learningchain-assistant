### Blockchain Awareness Programme at BRAOU

Sapphirus conducted a **Blockchain Awareness Programme at Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Open University (BRAOU)** from **21–24 January 2026**, bringing together our Implementation and Blockchain teams to engage with participants on blockchain technology and its practical applications.

The programme covered **blockchain architecture, core concepts and key benefits**, with interactive sessions that gave participants the opportunity to raise questions and engage directly with the Sapphirus team.

##### From Concepts to Practical Demonstration

Moving beyond concepts, our teams presented **live product demonstrations aligned with BRAOU workflows**, helping participants understand how blockchain-based solutions can support real-world academic and institutional requirements.

The sessions combined technology awareness, practical demonstrations and interactive discussions to provide participants with a clearer understanding of blockchain and its potential applications.

Demonstrating solutions in the context of BRAOU workflows and institutional requirements.

### YCMOU Launches Blockchain-Secured Degree Certificates with LearningChain

**Yashwantrao Chavan Maharashtra Open University (YCMOU)** launched blockchain-secured degree certificates using the **LearningChain platform** during its **29th Convocation**, held on 20 December 2023 at its Nashik campus.

The initiative brought blockchain technology into the academic credentialing process, supporting the issuance of **secure and verifiable digital degree certificates**.

##### Strengthening Trust in Academic Credentials

Through LearningChain, blockchain technology supports the authenticity and verification of academic credentials, helping create a more trusted digital credential ecosystem.

The launch marked an important milestone for the teams involved in bringing the initiative from implementation to real-world use

##### A Collaborative Milestone

We sincerely thank **YCMOU and everyone involved in the initiative** for their collaboration and contribution towards making this milestone possible.

### State-Level Summit on Blockchain-Powered Transcripts
