### Bid Manager

[Website](https://sapphirus.in) **Sapphirus PVT LTD**

Department: Sales / Business Development

Job Location: Not specified in the source

Required Experience: 5 -10 Years

We are looking for an experienced and detail-oriented Bid Manager to lead the end-to-end bid management process, including RFP/RFI responses, proposal development, tender submissions, and coordination with internal stakeholders. The ideal candidate will have strong proposal-writing, project management, commercial, and communication skills, with the ability to deliver high-quality, compliant, and competitive bids within strict deadlines.

Key Responsibilities

· Manage the complete bid lifecycle, from opportunity identification and qualification to submission and post-submission follow-up.

· Review and analyze RFPs, RFIs, RFQs, tenders, and other client requirements.

· Coordinate with Sales, Business Development, Delivery, Finance, Legal, HR, and technical teams to gather relevant inputs.

· Develop compelling proposals, bid responses, executive summaries, presentations, and supporting documentation.

· Prepare bid plans, submission schedules, compliance matrices, and responsibility assignments.

· Ensure all bid documents meet client requirements, quality standards, and submission deadlines.

· Manage pricing inputs, commercial proposals, costing, and approvals in coordination with relevant stakeholders.

· Identify risks, gaps, and opportunities to improve proposal competitiveness.

· Maintain a repository of proposal templates, case studies, company credentials, and reusable content.

· Track bid progress, maintain bid status reports, and provide regular updates to management.

· Conduct post-bid reviews and maintain records of win/loss analysis.

· Ensure confidentiality, accuracy, and consistency across all submitted documents.

Required Skills and Qualifications

· Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Marketing, Engineering, or a related discipline.

· 5–10 years of experience in bid management, proposal management, tender management.

· Proven experience managing RFP/RFI/RFQ responses and complex proposal submissions.

· Excellent written and verbal communication skills.

· Strong business writing, editing, documentation, and presentation skills.

· Experience coordinating with cross-functional teams and senior stakeholders.

· Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple bids simultaneously.

· Proficiency in MS Office, Excel, PowerPoint, and document management tools.

· Understanding of commercial proposals, pricing, compliance requirements, and contract documentation.

· Mandatory government tenders, e-procurement portals, and public-sector bidding, where applicable

Preferred Qualifications

· Experience in IT services, software development, consulting, or technology companies.

· Exposure to India bidding processes.

· Knowledge of government tenders, e-procurement portals, and public-sector bidding, where applicable.

· Familiarity with CRM tools, proposal automation platforms, and AI-based proposal development tools.

· Experience tracking bid performance metrics and improving win rates.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

· On-time submission rate.

· Bid compliance and documentation accuracy.

· Proposal quality and stakeholder satisfaction.

· Bid-to-win ratio.

· Turnaround time for proposal preparation.

· Effectiveness of bid planning and coordination.

Ideal Candidate Profile

The ideal candidate is proactive, commercially aware, detail-oriented, and capable of managing multiple high-priority bids independently while collaborating effectively with sales, technical, and leadership teams.
