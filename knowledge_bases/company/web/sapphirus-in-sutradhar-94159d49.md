# Sutradhar | Sapphirus Systems Pvt Ltd

Source: https://sapphirus.in/sutradhar/
Last updated: 2026-11-03

Connecting Data. Strengthening Governance. Enabling Smarter Decisions.

Sutradhar is a data-driven governance solution designed to help government bodies and public institutions consolidate information from multiple sources, identify inconsistencies and improve administrative decision-making.

By bringing together departmental records, field information and analytical insights, Sutradhar helps authorities gain clearer visibility into operations, identify areas requiring attention and support more informed governance.

 **Product Highlights**

### Unified Data Management

Bring together information from multiple departments, databases and operational sources into a consolidated view.

### Intelligent Data Analytics

Analyse institutional and administrative information to identify trends, patterns and potential discrepancies.

### Property & Revenue Record Analysis

Compare property records with relevant survey and assessment information to highlight possible inconsistencies.

### GIS & Survey Data Integration

Support the comparison of geographical, drone-survey and existing administrative information where applicable.

### Discrepancy Identification

Flag mismatches, missing information and unusual patterns for further examination by authorised officials.

### Interactive Dashboards & Reporting

Present relevant information through visual dashboards and reports to support monitoring, review and planning.

Municipal corporations, urban local bodies, government departments, public administration authorities and institutions managing complex operational data.

#### Blockchain Secure Financial Management System (BFMS)

BFMS is a financial data management and reconciliation solution designed to help institutions monitor financial transactions, compare records across systems and identify potential discrepancies.

By combining structured financial information, analytical checks and blockchain-backed record integrity, BFMS aims to support more transparent financial monitoring and informed administrative review.

 **Product Highlights**

### Consolidated Financial Data

Bring together relevant billing, collection, payment and financial records for structured analysis.

### Automated Record Reconciliation

Compare transaction information across different financial sources to identify mismatches.

### Discrepancy & Exception Detection

Flag differences in amounts, missing entries and unusual transaction patterns for authorised review.

### Rule-Based Financial Analysis

Apply defined business rules to identify transactions that require further examination.

### Blockchain-Backed Record Integrity

Support tamper-evident verification of approved financial records through cryptographic mechanisms.

### Financial Dashboards & Reporting

Provide visibility into collections, reconciliation exceptions and relevant financial indicators.

Consider an institution where a payment collection record shows ₹10,000, while the corresponding financial register reflects ₹8,000.

BFMS can be configured to compare the records and flag the ₹2,000 difference for investigation. The discrepancy is an exception requiring review, not automatic evidence of wrongdoing.

Universities, government departments, municipal authorities, public-sector institutions and organisations managing financial collections and reconciliation processes.
