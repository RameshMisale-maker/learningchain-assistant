### Sales Executive

[Website](https://sapphirus.in) **Sapphirus PVT LTD**

Department: Sales & Marketing

Job Location: Hyderabad

Required Experience: 1-4 Years

Job Summary

Generate and qualify sales leads, understand customer requirements, promote company products and services, and convert prospects into customers through effective communication, product demonstrations, and follow-ups.

Roles & Responsibilities

· Identify potential customers through leads, referrals, research, and outbound calls.

· Contact prospects through calls and emails to promote company products and services.

· Understand customer requirements and qualify sales opportunities.

· Explain product features, benefits, and solutions to customers.

· Schedule product demonstrations and sales meetings.

· Prepare quotations and proposals and coordinate with internal teams.

· Follow up with prospects to convert leads into sales.

· Maintain customer information and sales updates in CRM.

· Build customer relationships and identify additional sales opportunities.

· Achieve assigned sales targets and submit regular reports.

Mandatory Skills

· Lead Generation & Lead Qualification

· Inside Sales / Telecalling / Telemarketing

· Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

· Excellent Verbal & Written Communication

· Sales Negotiation & Follow-ups

· Customer Handling & Objection Management

· MS Office, Excel, and Email Communication

Desired Candidate Profile

· Experience in Sales, Inside Sales, Telecalling, or Customer Engagement.

· Strong communication and convincing skills.

· Ability to understand customer needs and promote suitable solutions.

· Good time management and problem-solving skills.

· Ability to manage multiple leads and achieve sales targets.

Performance Metrics

· Qualified leads generated.

· Product demonstrations scheduled.

· Lead-to-sales conversion ratio.

· Follow-up effectiveness.

· Achievement of sales targets.

· CRM accuracy and reporting
