# Label Secure | Sapphirus Systems Pvt Ltd

Source: https://sapphirus.in/label-secure/
Last updated: 2026-11-09

Protect Product Authenticity. Build Consumer Trust.

LABEL Secure is a blockchain-enabled product authentication solution designed to help businesses protect product identity, strengthen brand credibility and support verification of genuine products.

By connecting physical product labels with digital verification information, LABEL Secure helps manufacturers, brands and consumers establish greater confidence in product authenticity while supporting transparency across product verification processes.

## Product Highlights

### Unique Product Identification

Associate individual products or batches with unique digital identification information.

### QR-Based Product Verification

Enable users to scan product labels and access relevant authenticity information.

### Blockchain-Backed Authenticity

Use blockchain-supported verification mechanisms to help protect the integrity of registered product information.

### Anti-Counterfeiting Support

Help brands identify potentially unauthorised or unregistered product labels through digital verification.

### Product Information Traceability

Support access to relevant product details and verification history, depending on the information captured.

### Centralised Product Management

Enable businesses to manage product identification records and associated verification information through a structured system.

#### About LABEL Secure

LABEL Secure connects physical product labels with digital authentication to help businesses strengthen product verification and brand protection.

Designed for organisations seeking greater confidence in product authenticity, the solution supports unique product identification, digital verification and blockchain-backed record integrity. By making relevant product information accessible through connected labels, LABEL Secure helps businesses build trust with customers and support more transparent product authentication processes.
