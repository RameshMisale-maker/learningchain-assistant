# ID Secure | Sapphirus Systems Pvt Ltd

Source: https://sapphirus.in/id-secure/
Last updated: 2026-11-09

ID Secure helps organisations strengthen identity verification by connecting physical identification credentials with digital authentication processes.

Designed for institutions that require reliable identity management, the solution supports QR-based verification, credential integrity and structured access to identity information. It helps organisations move beyond conventional identification cards towards more connected and verifiable identity systems.

## Product Highlights

#### Digital Identity Management

Maintain structured identity information for authorised institutional use.

#### QR-Enabled Identity Verification

Enable verification of identity credentials through scannable QR codes.

#### Blockchain-Backed Authenticity

Support the integrity and authenticity verification of issued identity credentials.

#### Physical & Digital Identity Connectivity

Connect physical identification cards with corresponding digital records.

#### Centralised Credential Management

Support institutional processes for issuing and managing identity credentials.

#### System Integration Capabilities

Enable connectivity with relevant institutional databases and applications, subject to implementation requirements.

**Universities, colleges, educational institutions, government organisations and enterprises managing identity credentials.**

# Green Smart Card

Green Smart Card is a blockchain-enabled student identification solution that combines a physical smart identity card with digital verification capabilities.

Designed for educational institutions, it uses QR-based authentication and a connected database to help verify student identity information through a convenient digital process.

#### Product Highlights

#### Blockchain-Enabled Student ID

Associate issued student identification credentials with blockchain-backed verification mechanisms.

#### QR-Based Instant Verification

Scan the QR code to initiate verification of the cardholder's identity information.

#### Physical & Digital Connectivity

Connect a physical PVC identification card with its corresponding digital record.

#### Centralised Student Database

Maintain relevant student identity information through a web-based management system.

#### Credential Authenticity Verification

Help institutions determine whether a presented identity credential corresponds to an authorised record.

#### API Integration Support

Support connectivity with relevant institutional applications and databases, subject to technical requirements.

**Universities, colleges, schools and educational institutions seeking connected student identification and verification.**
