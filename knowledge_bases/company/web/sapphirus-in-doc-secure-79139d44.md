From Document Digitisation to Trusted Digital Records

DOC Secure is the document-focused solution family within BSecured, designed to help institutions digitise, organise, protect and verify important records throughout their lifecycle

## Two Solutions. Different Document Challenges.

#### Blockchain Document Management System (BDMS)

BDMS is a blockchain-enabled document management solution designed to help organisations digitise, organise, securely access and verify important records. It transforms fragmented physical and digital archives into structured, searchable document repositories.

#### Tabulation Register (TR)

TR is an AI and blockchain-powered solution designed to digitise historical university examination registers. It converts complex legacy records into structured, searchable academic information while maintaining human validation and blockchain-backed record integrity.

#### BDMS (Blockchain Document Management System)

**Document Digitisation**

Convert physical documents and legacy records into manageable digital formats.

**OCR-Assisted Data Extraction**

Extract relevant information from scanned documents to support indexing and retrieval.

**Smart Document Classification**

Organise records using structured categories, metadata and searchable attributes.

**Advanced Search & Retrieval**

Quickly locate relevant documents through indexed search capabilities.

**Role-Based Access & Audit Visibility**

Manage document access and support tracking of relevant activities.

**Blockchain-Backed Integrity Verification**

Use cryptographic verification mechanisms to help detect changes to approved records.

**Government departments, universities, public-sector organisations and enterprises managing large document volumes.**

#### Tabulation Register (TR)

**AI & OCR-Powered Extraction**

Extract student information, marks and examination results from supported historical registers.

**Complex Register Processing**

Process different examination layouts, tabular formats and legacy document structures.

**Exception Review & Human Validation**

Flag uncertain information and enable authorised staff to validate extracted records.

**Blockchain-Based Record Integrity**

Protect approved academic information through tamper-evident cryptographic verification.

**Instant Search & Retrieval**

Locate validated historical student records without manually searching physical registers.

**Academic Verification & Duplicate Issuance Support**

Retrieve approved information for historical verification and authorised duplicate academic record workflows.

**Universities, examination boards, Controllers of Examinations and academic record departments.**
