### Finance Lead

[Website](https://sapphirus.in) **Sapphirus PVT LTD**

**Department:** Finance and Accounts

**Location:**

**Key Responsibilities:**

We are seeking a qualified and experienced Finance Lead to head the organization’s accounting and finance

function. The incumbent will be accountable for the integrity of financial reporting, statutory and regulatory

compliance, treasury and cash-flow management, budgeting and financial planning, taxation, and internal

controls. The role requires a hands-on finance leader with strong technical foundations, sound commercial

acumen, and proven team-management capability. The Finance Lead will act as a trusted business partner to

senior leadership and ensure the finance function enables informed decision-making and sustainable growth.


**Key Responsibilities:**

- · Lead and manage the end-to-end accounting and finance function, overseeing day-to-day
- operations, month-end close, and timely delivery of all financial outputs.
- · Oversee preparation and finalization of monthly, quarterly, and annual financial statements
- Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss Statement, and Cash Flow Statement in accordance with
- applicable accounting standards.
- · Ensure accuracy, completeness, and integrity of accounting records, books, and general
- ledger across all legal entities.
- · Own statutory, tax, and regulatory compliance — including Income Tax, GST, TDS, PF,
- ESI, Professional Tax, ROC filings, and related obligations.
- · Manage direct and indirect taxation — computation, filings, assessments, refunds, and
- reconciliations; coordinate with tax consultants and authorities as required.
- · Drive the annual budgeting process, periodic forecasting, variance analysis, and financial
- planning; provide actionable insights and recommendations to leadership.
- · Oversee treasury and cash-flow management — banking operations, liquidity planning, fund
- deployment, working-capital management, and banker relationships.
- · Lead internal, statutory, and tax audits; coordinate with auditors, respond to audit queries,
- and ensure timely closure of audit observations.
- · Design, implement, and continuously strengthen internal financial controls, accounting
- policies, SOPs, and the overall governance framework.
- · Review receivables and payables management, vendor reconciliations, employee
- reimbursements, and expense governance.
- · Deliver MIS reports, dashboards, financial analyses, and commercial evaluations to support
- strategic decision-making by senior management.
- · Evaluate pricing, costing, margins, and commercial terms for projects, proposals, and major
- transactions.
- · Manage relationships with banks, auditors, tax consultants, regulators, and other external
- stakeholders on behalf of the organization.
- · Partner with cross-functional teams — Sales, Operations, HR, Procurement — on budgeting,
- approvals, costing, and commercial matters.
- · Build, mentor, and develop a strong finance team; drive capability building, process automation, digitization, and continuous improvement across the function.


**Qualification:** Chartered Accountant (CA) is good to have. Candidates with CA Inter (Level 1 cleared) will be considered favorably. Additional qualifications such as CPA, CMA, or an MBA in Finance from a reputed institute will be an added advantage.

**Experience:**

- · 8+ years of progressive experience in accounting and finance, with at least 3–4 years in a finance leadership / team-management role.
- · Strong hands-on expertise in financial reporting, finalization of accounts, and applicable accounting standards (Ind-AS / IFRS).
- · Hands-on experience with US financial reporting and US accounting / tax exposure (US GAAP, US Federal & State filings, 1099/W-2, Sales & Use Tax) is required.
- · In-depth knowledge of direct and indirect taxation — Income Tax, GST, TDS — and statutory compliances.
- · Proven experience in budgeting, forecasting, financial planning, and variance analysis.
- · Solid exposure to treasury operations, cash-flow management, working-capital management, and banking relationships.
- · Experience in leading internal, statutory, and tax audits, and in designing and strengthening internal financial controls.
- · Strong working knowledge of ERP and accounting systems (QuickBooks — required; Tally, Zoho Books) and advanced proficiency in MS Excel and financial modelling.
- · Excellent leadership, stakeholder-management, communication, and team-building skills.
- · High integrity, analytical mindset, attention to detail, and commercial acumen.
- · Ability to work in a fast-paced, evolving environment and manage multiple priorities with competing deadlines.
