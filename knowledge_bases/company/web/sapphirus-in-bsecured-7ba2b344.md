## BSecured

Digital Trust Across Identities, Products and Documents.

BSecured is the Sapphirus platform family for identity verification, product authentication and trusted document management.

Its three product families ID Secure, LABEL Secure and DOC Secure address different authenticity and information-management needs.

### The Challenge

As organisations digitise their operations, they need to establish who or what is being verified, which record is authoritative and how information can be retrieved with confidence. These needs vary across student identity, consumer products and important institutional documents.

### Key Capabilities

### ID Secure

Supports identity-related verification. Its Green Smart Card offering combines physical student identification with blockchain-enabled QR verification.

### LABEL Secure

Addresses product identification and authenticity checking for brands and relevant supply-chain environments.

### DOC Secure

Supports document digitisation, retrieval and integrity workflows. Its product family includes BDMS and Tabulation Register digitisation.
