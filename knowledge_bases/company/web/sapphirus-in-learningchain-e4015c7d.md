## Trusted Academic Credentials. Simplified Verification.

LearningChain is a blockchain-powered digital credentialing solution designed to help educational institutions issue, manage, share and verify academic credentials. It connects institutional authority over records with convenient digital access for learners and verifiers.

### Academic records need more than digital storage.

Universities issue certificates, transcripts and academic records that may need verification years later. Manual searches, repeated employer requests and fragmented archival processes can increase administrative effort and delay responses. Digitising a certificate alone does not solve the need for dependable verification.

### Repeated Requests

Frequent enquiries to confirm academic records

### Fragmented Records

### Delays in response
