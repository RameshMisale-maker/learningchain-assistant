# LearningChain - Frequently Asked Questions (official website)

Product: LearningChain by Sapphirus - blockchain-powered academic credentialing platform.
Source: https://learningchain.in/faqs/

## FAQ: What is LearningChain?

LearningChain is an innovative online platform designed for educational institutions to issue tamper-proof and instantly verifiable digital certificates based on blockchain technology.

## FAQ: What is Blockchain Technology?

Blockchain is a digital ledger of transactions maintained by a network of computers, making it difficult to hack or alter. Each block in the chain contains a list of records linked using cryptography. Transactions are independently verified, time-stamped, and added to the chain, ensuring data integrity and security.

## FAQ: Why do we need LearningChain?

LearningChain addresses the issues of fraudulent activities, fake certificates, and delays in the verification process. By issuing blockchain-based digital certificates, LearningChain ensures that certificates are immutable, secure, and instantly verifiable worldwide, thus protecting the institution's brand and the students' future.

## FAQ: How does Blockchain make my documents tamper-proof?

Blockchain technology stores data across a decentralized network, making it nearly impossible for any single entity to tamper with the data without the consent of all network nodes.

## FAQ: Where does LearningChain store my data?

LearningChain stores data on Microsoft Azure, ensuring secure and reliable data storage.

## FAQ: How does LearningChain work?

Institution administrators upload scanned certificates in PDF format to the LearningChain portal. The system creates a unique hash value for the data, which is then recorded into a unique QR code. When scanned, the QR code allows verifiers to access and cross-check the original document's content with the blockchain-stamped data.

## FAQ: How is a Blockchain QR Code safer than a normal QR code?

Blockchain QR codes contain a unique hash value that is cross-checked with the blockchain when scanned. This ensures that the data is incorruptible and immutable. Unlike normal QR codes, which can be manipulated if the destination data is compromised, blockchain QR codes are highly secure.

## FAQ: What are Blockchain-based Digital Credentials?

Blockchain-based digital credentials are certificates generated using blockchain technology, ensuring they are tamper-proof and immutable.

## FAQ: What happens if I make changes to a document after sharing the QR code-embedded LearningChain document?

You need to generate a new QR code for the altered document to ensure it remains valid for verification.

## FAQ: What makes LearningChain's verification process different?

The traditional manual verification process is time-consuming and costly. LearningChain allows instant verification by uploading the key file and original document, significantly reducing staff hours and time delays.

## FAQ: Does LearningChain support different file formats for uploading in the blockchain process?

Currently, LearningChain only supports PDF formats for uploading certificates.

## FAQ: What is the validity of the data on the blockchain network using LearningChain?

Data uploaded to the blockchain network via LearningChain is valid for a lifetime.

## FAQ: Can we integrate LearningChain with other existing applications?

Yes, LearningChain can be integrated with other applications using APIs. However, this requires validating the technical details of the existing application.

## FAQ: Does LearningChain have control over the data?

No, LearningChain does not have access to or control over client data.

## FAQ: What is the difference between LearningChain and DigiLocker?

DIGILOCKER uses a centralized architecture for data storage and verification, making it vulnerable to central server failures and hacking. In contrast, LearningChain is decentralized, using multiple nodes to store data, making it highly secure and tamper-proof.

## FAQ: How will students access and share their records?

Students will receive QR codes via email and their mobile digital wallet. They can easily share their credentials using social media links directly from the LearningChain digital wallet.

## FAQ: How does LearningChain help in institute brand building?

By providing blockchain-based digital certificates, institutions show a commitment to their students' futures. Students sharing these credentials on social media enhance the institution's visibility and expand its reach.

## FAQ: Does the institution need to pay repeatedly to maintain records for a lifetime?

No, once a certificate is uploaded and pushed to the blockchain network, it remains there for a lifetime. Institutions only need to pay for the initial upload.
