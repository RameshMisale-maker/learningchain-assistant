## Why LearningChain

Article: https://learningchainai.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/26475444480665-Why-LearningChain

Updated: 2023-12-16

LearningChain is a Blockchain-powered Digital Certificate Issuance platform that enables institutions to move from a traditional manual certification process to a fully automated process for faster generation of digital certificates.

The Key Advantage of tamper-proof digital certificates enable institutes to minimize fraudulent certifications. Also, single-click verification of digital certificates puts a stop to time-consuming traditional manual verification enabling cost savings for verifiers and institutions.
