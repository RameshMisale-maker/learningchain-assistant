## About Us

Welcome to **LearningChain**, a groundbreaking product from the leading EdTech company, **Sapphirus Systems**, established in 2016 and headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana. Our mission is to set global standards for educational institutions by offering a robust platform to **issue tamper-proof digital certificates** that are globally verifiable and completely **secured using blockchain technology**.

##### Our Vision

At LearningChain, we recognize the pressing challenges facing the education system today. Fake certificates are on the rise worldwide, centralized data storage is vulnerable to failures and hacking, and the traditional certificate verification process is time-consuming and resource-intensive. To address these issues, LearningChain has emerged as a beacon of innovation, leveraging the power of blockchain technology.
