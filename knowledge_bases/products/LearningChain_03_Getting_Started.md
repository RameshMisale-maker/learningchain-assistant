## If You Forget Your Password ?

Article: https://learningchainai.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/26471667080601-If-You-Forget-Your-Password

Updated: 2025-03-25

(Video walkthrough: https://player.vimeo.com/video/1069105470)

- To reset the password, click on Forget password option on the login page.

- After clicking on Forget password, enter the genuine email id and click on Send.

- Now you will receive a password reset link in the mail, click on that link.

- Now enter the new password and re-enter the new password.

Note: Password must contain Up to 10 characters, 1 upper case letter, and 1 special character

  - After entering the new password click on the update button so that password will be updated and will show a Successful message.
